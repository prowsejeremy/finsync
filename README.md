# Hz

A native Android music and audiobook player that works entirely offline. Hz plays the audio files
in one Library folder on your phone and builds its whole library from their tags, artwork and
playlist files. An optional [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org) adapter syncs the music and books you
choose from your server into that folder, ready to play with no connection.

Hz started life as **Finsync**, a tool that only synced a Jellyfin library to the phone. It is now
a full player, and syncing is one adapter feeding it.

For how it's built and the rules behind it, see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Features

### The player

- **Your files, your library.** Everything in the Library folder (`Media/hz` by default) shows
  up, whether synced or copied in by hand. Albums, artists and genres come from the tags; covers
  from `folder.jpg` or the embedded art; artist photos from `artist.jpg`; playlists from `.m3u8`
  files.
- **Browse** Albums, Album Artists, Genres, Songs, Playlists and Audio Books from Home. Reorder or
  hide the categories in Settings → Home screen.
- **Playback** through the BASS audio engine: gapless albums, repeat and shuffle, a queue you can
  reorder and trim, Android's output switcher, and notification, lock-screen and Bluetooth
  controls. The queue is restored as you left it, even after a force-stop.
- **Audiobooks** with chapters, −15 s and +30 s skips, a pitch-preserving speed setting (0.8× to
  2.0×) and a saved position for every book.
- **A 10-band equaliser** with nine built-in presets, a Custom slot and presets saved under your
  own names. It applies to music and books alike.
- **Appearance:** Dark, Light or System mode, and a choice of six accent colours.
- **Formats:** MP3, FLAC, M4A/M4B (AAC and ALAC), Ogg, Opus, APE, WavPack, WAV and AIFF.

### The Jellyfin adapter

- **Sign in** with Quick Connect (approve a code from any signed-in Jellyfin app) or a username
  and password. Requires Jellyfin 12 or later. Hz opens and plays without signing in.
- **Choose what to sync:** albums, playlists and audiobooks.
- **Incremental sync.** Only new or changed items download, and each file is tagged with the
  server's details as it lands, along with album covers, artist photos and playlist files.
  Unchoosing something removes its files on the next sync.
- **Runs in the background** as a foreground service with a progress notification, and can repeat
  on a schedule with Auto-sync.
- **Read-only.** Hz never changes anything on your server.

## Getting started

1. Install the APK on an Android 8.0 (API 26) or later device.
2. Open the app and grant the storage and notification permissions it asks for.
3. **To play music already on your phone,** put it in `Media/hz`, or choose another folder in
   **Settings → Library**. Hz scans the folder when it opens; tap **Rescan** there after adding
   files.
4. **To sync from Jellyfin,** tap the round **Settings** button on Home, then **Adapters →
   Jellyfin → Sign in**. Enter your server's URL (e.g. `https://jellyfin.home.arpa` or
   `http://192.168.1.10:8096`), tap **Get code** and approve it in another Jellyfin app, or follow
   **Sign in with a username and password**.
5. On the Jellyfin page, pick **Albums to Sync**, **Playlists to Sync** and **Books to Sync**,
   then tap **Sync Now**. Set a schedule under **Auto-sync** if you want one.

Synced files land in their own folder inside the Library folder, named after your server:

```
Media/hz/<server name>/
├── Music/<album artist>/<album>/
├── Audiobooks/<author>/<title>/
└── Playlists/
```

## Building

Requires JDK 17, plus NDK 28.2.13676358 and CMake 3.22.1 from the Android SDK for the tag engine.
From the repo root:

```sh
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug                             # debug APK
./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest --rerun --console=plain # unit tests
```

See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#build-test-and-alpha-builds) for the full list of commands,
testing notes and alpha builds.

## Known limitations

- **No streaming.** Music must be in the Library folder to play; Jellyfin items play once synced.
- **No transcoding.** Files download in their original format.
- **No search** yet.
- **Jellyfin is the only adapter** so far, with one server at a time.
- **Large libraries** take significant time and storage on first sync; later syncs are incremental.
